The Energy Warning prevents you from accidentally wasting energy when using consumable items. If an item would push
your energy past the 1000E limit, the extension warns you before use.

## How It Works

When you click an energy-granting item, the extension checks:

1. Your **current energy** (from the sidebar energy bar)
2. The item's **energy value** (from Torn's item data)

If `current energy + item energy > 1000`, it triggers a two-layer warning:

### Inline Warning

An orange warning text appears near the item description:

> **Warning! Using this item increases your energy to over 1000!**

This is visible immediately when you select the item — before you even try
to use it.

### Confirmation Dialog

When you click **Use**, a browser confirmation dialog appears:

> **Are you sure to use this item? It will get you to more than 1000E.**

- **Cancel** — the item use is blocked
- **OK** — the item is used (you've confirmed you want to proceed anyway)

### What Items Are Covered

The warning works for **any item** that grants energy, including:

- Energy Drinks
- Drugs
- Feathery Hotel Coupons
- Any other consumable with an "Increases energy by X" effect

<Aside type="note">
	The Quick Items toolbar version is less restrictive: it only warns for items of type **Drug** or **Energy Drink**, and only when your current energy is
	already above your maximum. The items page and faction armory versions warn for all energy-granting items regardless of type.
</Aside>

## Where It Works

| Page                                | Items Covered                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Items page**                      | Any energy-granting item in your inventory        |
| **Faction armory** (`factions.php`) | Any energy-granting item in your faction's armory |
| **Quick Items on the items page**   | Energy Drinks and Drugs                           |

## Settings

| Setting                              | Default | Description                                |
| ------------------------------------ | ------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `settings.pages.items.energyWarning` | On      | Warn if an item gives you energy over 1000 |
